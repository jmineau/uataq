Getting started
===============

If you are new to uataq, read these pages in order.

1. :doc:`installation` installs uataq on CHPC, for a terminal, Jupyter on Open
   OnDemand, or a Slurm job.
2. :doc:`first_data` reads a month of CO2 and CH4 from one site, puts it in
   local time, averages it to hourly means and plots its daily cycle.
