Columns#

Note

The examples on this page read the UATAQ archive, which is only available on CHPC. They are shown as code and are not run when the documentation is built. The output under each one is from runs on CHPC on 12 October 2026. If the data are reprocessed, the numbers can change a little.

The columns you get depend on the instrument and on the data level you read (raw, qaqc, calibrated or final; see The four levels). This page says what the columns are, level by level, for the instruments you are most likely to use. Each section says which instrument and level its examples come from.

The examples print wide tables. These two pandas settings print them in full, whatever the width of your terminal:

>>> import pandas as pd
>>> import uataq
>>> pd.set_option("display.width", 120)
>>> pd.set_option("display.max_columns", None)

In every table#

  • The index is Time_UTC, the time of the measurement in UTC, without a time zone attached. Your first data shows how to convert it to local time.

  • The concentration columns follow one naming pattern for every instrument, level and research group: CO2d_ppm_cal, O3_ppb, PM2.5_ugm3 and so on. Column names explains the pattern.

  • uataq renames many of the instruments’ other columns to common names, so that a quantity has the same name across instruments and groups. For example, the lgr_ugga’s cavity pressure is Cavity_P_torr in the pipeline’s files and Internal_P_torr in uataq. The renaming is in lin.column_mapping and horel.column_mapping. Keep this in mind when you read the pipeline’s own documentation, which uses the names in its files.

Column names#

Concentration columns follow {pollutant}[d|channel]_{units}[_cal|_raw]: O3_ppb, CO2d_ppm_cal (dry mole fraction, calibrated), CH4d_ppm_raw, PM2.5_ugm3, BC6_ngm3 (aethalometer channel 6).

import uataq
from uataq.pollutants import concentration_columns, parse_column

obs = uataq.get_obs("WBB", "CO2", time_range="2024-01")
obs[concentration_columns("CO2", obs.columns)]  # CO2d_ppm_cal, not diagnostics
parse_column("CH4d_ppm_raw")  # pollutant='CH4', units='ppm', dry=True, calibration='raw'

The two functions are documented on the uataq.pollutants page.

_cal vs _raw: in lin final data _cal is the calibrated value and _raw the uncalibrated one, except for TRX01’s lgr_ugga_manual_cal. That exception, and the rows where _cal is empty, are explained under When _cal is empty.

Labels, units and expected ranges for plotting are the same for every instrument, so uataq leaves them to lair, the group’s atmospheric-science toolkit: lair.pollutants.get_pollutant("CH4").label().

The CO2 and CH4 analyzers#

This section is about the lin group’s greenhouse gas analyzers: the lgr_ugga (a Los Gatos Research analyzer, which measures CO2 and CH4) and the licor_6262 and licor_7000 (LI-COR analyzers, which measure CO2 only). They are the only instruments with a calibrated level. Each row is one record of the analyzer, about every 10 seconds. The examples read the lgr_ugga at WBB on 2 June 2025 and the licor_7000 at SUG on the same day.

What each level holds, and which rows final keeps, is on Data levels and flags.

Final#

>>> final = uataq.read_data("WBB", "lgr_ugga", time_range="2025-06-02")["lgr_ugga"]
>>> final.head(3)
                     CO2d_ppm_cal  CO2d_ppm_raw  CH4d_ppm_cal  CH4d_ppm_raw   H2O_ppm
Time_UTC
2025-06-02 00:00:01    431.852558      434.8026      1.981142      1.979105  7088.775
2025-06-02 00:00:11    431.612346      434.5594      1.980728      1.978685  7101.099
2025-06-02 00:00:21    431.812938      434.7615      1.980816      1.978773  7150.829

Column

Meaning

CO2d_ppm_cal

CO2 dry mole fraction in ppm, calibrated against the site’s reference tanks. Can be empty; see When _cal is empty.

CO2d_ppm_raw

The same measurement before calibration.

CH4d_ppm_cal, CH4d_ppm_raw

The same for CH4, in ppm (lgr_ugga only).

H2O_ppm

Water vapor in ppm, taken from the qaqc level.

The licor_7000 has the same columns without the CH4 ones:

>>> uataq.read_data("SUG", "licor_7000", time_range="2025-06-02")["licor_7000"].columns.tolist()
['CO2d_ppm_cal', 'CO2d_ppm_raw', 'H2O_ppm']

get_obs() returns the concentration columns of this level, so get_obs("WBB", "CO2") gives CO2d_ppm_cal and CO2d_ppm_raw.

The pipeline calibrates each analyzer with the reference tanks it measures every few hours: it fits a straight line between the tanks’ measured and known values, for every row. The pipeline’s calibration page describes the method. The fit columns, for CO2 (the CH4 ones start with CH4d_):

Column

Meaning

CO2d_ppm_raw

The uncalibrated value: the qaqc level’s CO2d_ppm. Empty on flagged rows (all 8,362 flush rows at WBB in June 2025), except rows flagged for an out-of-range reading, which keep it.

CO2d_m, CO2d_b

The slope and intercept of the fit. The calibrated value is CO2d_ppm_cal = (CO2d_ppm_raw - CO2d_b) / CO2d_m.

CO2d_n

How many reference tanks the fit used at that time. With one tank the fit is a slope only (CO2d_b is 0). With none, there is no fit and CO2d_ppm_cal is empty.

CO2d_rsq, CO2d_rmse

The fit’s R² and root mean square error (in ppm). Empty when the fit used fewer than three tanks.

ID_CO2, ID_CH4

What the analyzer was sampling; see What the analyzer was sampling.

QAQC_Flag

The qaqc level’s flag, unless the calibration flagged the row itself (see Flags).

The formula holds to rounding:

>>> ((cal["CO2d_ppm_raw"] - cal["CO2d_b"]) / cal["CO2d_m"] - cal["CO2d_ppm_cal"]).abs().max()
np.float64(2.8421709430404007e-12)

QAQC#

>>> qaqc = uataq.read_data("WBB", "lgr_ugga", lvl="qaqc", time_range="2025-06-02")["lgr_ugga"]
>>> qaqc.columns.tolist()
['CH4_ppm', 'CH4_ppm_sd', 'H2O_ppm', 'H2O_ppm_sd', 'CO2_ppm', 'CO2_ppm_sd', 'CH4d_ppm', 'CH4d_ppm_sd', 'CO2d_ppm', 'CO2d_ppm_sd', 'Internal_P_torr', 'Internal_P_torr_sd', 'Internal_T_C', 'Internal_T_C_sd', 'Ambient_T_C', 'Ambient_T_C_sd', 'RingDown0_us', 'RingDown0_us_sd', 'RingDown1_us', 'RingDown1_us_sd', 'Fit_Flag', 'ID', 'ID_CO2', 'ID_CH4', 'QAQC_Flag']

Column

Meaning

CO2_ppm, CH4_ppm, H2O_ppm

The mole fractions in the sampled (wet) air, in ppm, not calibrated.

CO2d_ppm, CH4d_ppm

The dry mole fractions, not calibrated. They are the _raw values of the calibrated and final levels.

*_sd

A standard deviation that the lgr_ugga’s software writes beside each value ([CO2]d_ppm_sd in the analyzer’s own files). The LI-COR analyzers have none.

Fit_Flag

A status number that the lgr_ugga’s software writes with each row. uataq and the pipeline pass it through unchanged, and the pipeline’s QAQC does not use it. Every row of this day has 3.

ID, ID_CO2, ID_CH4

What the analyzer was sampling; see What the analyzer was sampling.

QAQC_Flag

The pipeline’s flag; see Flags.

Internal_*, Ambient_T_C, RingDown*

The analyzer’s own measurements of its state (cavity pressure and temperature, and so on), with their _sd.

The licor_6262 and licor_7000 have other instrument columns at this level (flow, pressure, temperature, the logger’s battery voltage). Print columns to see them. Their Analog_H2O_ppth is the analyzer’s own water vapor reading, in parts per thousand (not ppm like H2O_ppm, which the pipeline computes from the cavity’s humidity and pressure).

Other instruments#

The lin group’s other instruments (the Teledyne analyzers, the 2B ozone monitor, the MetOne particle monitor, and so on) have no calibrated level and no _cal or _raw columns. Their qaqc level has the instrument’s columns and QAQC_Flag; their final level keeps the rows that pass QAQC and only a few columns. For the teledyne_t400 (ozone) at WBB on 2 June 2025:

>>> ozone = uataq.read_data("WBB", "teledyne_t400", lvl="qaqc", time_range="2025-06-02")["teledyne_t400"]
>>> ozone.columns.tolist()
['O3_ppb_std', 'O3_Meas_mV', 'O3_Ref_mV', 'O3_Gen_mV', 'O3_Drive_mV', 'Photo_Power_mV', 'Internal_P_inHgA', 'Flow_mLpm', 'Internal_T_C', 'Photo_Lamp_T_C', 'O3_Scrub_T_C', 'O3_Gen_T_C', 'Instrument_T_C', 'Slope', 'Offset', 'O3_ppb', 'Test_mV', 'QAQC_Flag']
>>> uataq.read_data("WBB", "teledyne_t400", time_range="2025-06-02")["teledyne_t400"].columns.tolist()
['O3_ppb']

The pipeline lists the columns of every instrument and level in its data_config.json, with the names in its files, before uataq renames them.

The horel group’s instruments#

The TRAX trains and the buses also carry instruments run by the horel group (ozone, particles, NOx; see Research group). Their qaqc and final levels are read from the same horel files, and their columns are named like the lin group’s. The qaqc level of the ozone monitor (2b_205) on TRX01 on 1 June 2024 has:

>>> o3 = uataq.read_data("TRX01", "2b_205", lvl="qaqc", group="horel", time_range="2024-06-01")["2b_205"]
>>> o3.columns.tolist()
['O3_ppb', 'Flow_Lpm', 'Internal_T_C', 'Internal_P_hPa', 'QAQC_Flag']

Their flags are 0 or -1 (see Flags).
