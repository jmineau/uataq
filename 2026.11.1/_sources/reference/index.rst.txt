Reference
=========

What the sites, columns and arguments are, written once and linked from the
other pages.

- :doc:`sites_and_instruments`: every site and instrument, what each measures,
  their dates, and a glossary
- :doc:`columns`: the columns of each instrument and data level, and their
  names
- :doc:`input_parameters`: the arguments of the reading functions: site,
  instruments, pollutants, format, group, level, time range, processes, how
  far back to read, file pattern and GPS
- :doc:`config`: the keys of uataq's configuration file
- :doc:`functions_and_modules`: every function, class and module, from their
  docstrings
